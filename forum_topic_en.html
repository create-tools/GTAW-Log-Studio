Overview & Purpose

GTAW Log Studio is a standalone desktop application that hooks into the FiveM client process to capture, categorize, colorize, and locally archive in-game chatlogs in real time with zero performance impact.

It automatically backs up your gaming sessions so you never lose chat logs due to unexpected game crashes. Instead of manually parsing messy text files, you can filter and review IC speech, actions (/me, /do), private messages (PM), radio broadcasts, and SMS logs formatted with official GTA World color codes.

Additionally, the built-in standalone SS Studio allows you to import your in-game screenshots directly onto the canvas, position dialogues, customize typography, outlines, shadows, and word-level censorship to produce finished roleplay screenshots without even needing Photoshop. For players who prefer external editors, one-click transparent PNG clipboard export and /ssmod black chatbox luma-keying tools are also readily available. All operations run 100% locally on your machine with full privacy.

Core Features

  • Live Memory Hook: Real-time filtering for IC dialogue, /me, /do, PMs, Radio, and SMS channels.
  • 1-Click In-App Auto-Updater: Direct in-app stream downloads with smooth visual transition screens.
  • In-Place Portable Updater: Updates standalone .exe directly in its folder without setup wizards.
  • Single Instance Lock & Data Migration: Eliminates zombie background processes and preserves all past sessions and disk settings.
  • Standalone SS Studio: Full-featured canvas with word-level censorship, direct screenshot imports and instant image exports.
  • Black Background Keyer: Cleans solid black /ssmod chatbox screenshots into transparent text layers.
  • Radio & Dispatch Terminal: Dedicated LEO/EMS filter for 10-codes, broadcasts, and callsigns.
  • Multi-POV Merger: Chronologically interleaves chatlogs from multiple players and deduplicates identical lines.
  • Smart Alt-Tab Alerts: Audio chimes sound only when mentioned or PMed while the game is unfocused.
  • 5 Languages: English, Turkish, Russian, French, and Spanish.
